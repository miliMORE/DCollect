How to use the files

  • Complete file — one zip with the county workbook and every uploaded document, in folders.
  • County pack — the Excel workbook only (questionnaire, jobs, staff forms, benefits, checklist, and for reviewers market position).
  • Progress — who has submitted and how complete each module is.
  • Questionnaire extract — every institutional answer, one row per county.
  • Individual responses — every staff form, including job group.
  • Market workbook — basic and total cash position, SRC roll-ups, and comparator medians.
  • Document register — one row per uploaded file; use Download files for the documents themselves.
