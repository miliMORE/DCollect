Sign in with the account issued to you. Work only on the pages in the left menu. If a page is missing, that is because it is not part of your role.

Administrator

You prepare the survey and keep it moving. Counties cannot start until files and logins exist.

  1. Open Variables & settings. Confirm the survey title, support email, confidentiality text, maximum upload size and the active survey period.
  2. Under catalogues, check counties, benchmark jobs, benefits, document types and dropdown lists. If a job is missing, add it here before counties start filling. Do not ask them to type their own job titles.
  3. Open Forms & questions. Mark which questionnaire items, jobs, benefits and documents are required. Add extra questions to a section if the standard form is not enough. Counties see a Required mark on those items.
  4. On Comparators, open Details and delete an organisation if it should not stay in the market set. That removes its stored pay and benefits. County files are not touched.
  5. On Variables & settings, open a file for selected counties, or for every county.
  6. Go to Users. Create one county respondent for each county, or use Create missing county logins. Add the consultants who will review files and the analysts who will download reports.
  7. On Variables & settings or County files, open a file for selected counties, or for every county. Safe to click again — existing files are left alone.
  8. Open a county file if you need to assign a named consultant.
  9. Use the dashboard to see drafts, submissions and clarifications. When a file is validated, the analysts can use Analysis and Reports.
  10. Open Audit trail to see what every user did, with the time, the account, the county file and the network address — including account edits, deactivations, deletions and password changes. Collection administrators cannot open this page. The Activity list on a county file is still the working history for that file only.
  11. Open Data administration to delete test data for one county or one user, or to reset all collected answers. Catalogues and logins stay. Deleting a login under Users does not remove answers they already entered, and their audit rows stay.
  12. Open Staff survey links. Copy the Kisumu link (or any county) and send it to people who work there but do not have a county login. Their forms land on that county’s Individual responses page.
  13. Download the full user manual (PDF) from the left menu. It includes navigation steps and screen figures for every role.

County respondent

You complete the file for {% if user.county %}{{ user.county.name }}{% else %}your county{% endif %} only. Keep the salary structure and the allowance and benefits schedules next to you.

  1. On the dashboard, open My county file. If a Next step box is showing, start there.
  2. Questionnaire — work through A to J. Save a section before you leave it. Staff totals on section B add themselves. County and survey period are already filled. In section E, say whether medical insurance is provided and how the county arranges cover (SHA, private, mixed, self-funded, reimbursement or other).
  3. Jobs — complete one row per job group (A–U, excluding I and O). Enter the SRC equivalent as text (for example EX4), then min/max basic, house, commuter, airtime and total of other allowances. Mark N/A if the county does not use that group.
  4. After you submit, you cannot change answers unless the reviewer asks a clarification. You may then edit only the section they asked about.
  5. Benefits — mark each item Yes, No or Partial. Note who contributes and who is eligible.
  6. Documents — upload the supporting files. You may attach several files under one heading (for example more than one salary structure). A required heading needs at least one file. A tick without a file is not enough.
  7. Certify — your name, designation and initials. That is required before submit if the survey is set that way.
  8. Back on the file overview, Submit for review. After that you cannot edit until a reviewer sends the file back.
  9. If Clarifications appear, answer each one, correct the relevant section, and submit again.

You may download a county pack of your own answers for your records. It is not the market comparison.

Field consultant

You check that a county file can be used. Start with files that are submitted or have been sent back.

  1. Dashboard → Needs attention, or open County files and filter by Submitted / Clarification.
  2. Read the questionnaire, then jobs, benefits and the uploaded documents. Check that the structure and supporting files match the figures.
  3. Fill the Validation checklist. Use the comments where something is incomplete or looks off.
  4. If the county must correct something, send a Clarification and name the module. That returns the file to them.
  5. When the file is in order, Mark validated.
  6. Open Comparators. Enter pay and benefits for the same jobs the counties used. Only organisations marked for the market median are used in Analysis. A platform administrator can delete a comparator from its Details page — that also removes its pay and benefits figures.
  7. On a county file, open Individual responses to see staff who submitted through that county’s survey link.
{% endif %} {% if role == 'analyst' or role == 'consultant' or user.is_admin_role %}

Analyst

You work after files have been validated and comparator pay has been entered. You do not fill county files.

  1. Open Analysis. Pick a county and Basic or Total cash. Each row is a job group. County P25 / P50 / P75 are the official midpoint plus any staff forms for that group (quartiles need two or more figures). Market P25 / P50 / P75 are percentiles of comparator organisation medians. Use By SRC equivalent to see groups that map to the same SRC code (for example EX4).
  2. Hide empty groups if a county has not filled a row. Download the market workbook for every county at once.
  3. On a county file, use Download complete file for one zip: the county workbook and every uploaded document, arranged in folders. Open Documents to download a single file, or documents only.
  4. Open Reports and download what you need:
    • County pack — one county’s full file.
    • Collection progress — status of every county.
    • Questionnaire extract — institutional answers, one row per county.
    • Job group pay — min/max, SRC equivalent and allowances by group.
    • Benefits matrix — every county’s benefits answers.
    • Document register — what was uploaded or marked not available.
    • Market position — the comparison across counties.
    • Individual responses — staff survey forms.

The official county figure is the midpoint of min and max. Market percentiles are taken from comparator organisations included in the market median. A leftover payroll median on a county row is used only if min and max are both blank.

If something will not work

  • After submit, the county file is locked. The county can edit again only in sections where a clarification was requested.
  • A county login cannot open another county’s file.
  • Jobs, benefits or document types added in settings appear the next time that file is opened.
  • If you cannot sign in, contact the person who issued the account. Use Change password after the first login if you were asked to.
  • The system signs you out after 10 minutes without activity. You will need to sign in again. That sign-out is written to the audit trail.
